## About The Humanitarian OpenStreet Map Team (HOT)

Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT) is an NGO which supports a global community of over 750,000 volunteers who use maps and open data for humanitarian response and the Sustainable Development Goals. When a major disaster strikes, the HOT community mobilizes to create the maps that data responders need. HOT collaborates with partners such as the Red Cross, Médecins Sans Frontières, the World Bank, UN OCHA, and hundreds more to make sure these maps are used to make decisions which reduce human suffering. Over the past five years, the HOT community has been working on an ambitious goal: to map an area home to one billion people affected by disasters or multidimensional poverty. On this journey, they have mapped an area home to more than 900 million people and provided critical data for nearly 100 disaster responses. HOT’s model is open source, participatory, and community-led; HOT’s 70+ staff provide the tools, platforms, knowledge, and space for communities and individuals to map and solve local challenges. 

## About the Role

The Regional Director is HOT’s most senior representative in East and Southern Africa and is a member of HOT’s leadership team. The Regional Director must execute and iterate our strategy to expand the use of free, open, and accessible geospatial data across ESA. This involves leading our virtual hub team of 10 staff across four countries, delivering on our projects in the region, establishing and growing partnerships across government, NGOs, donors, and communities, and ensuring the team continues to innovate the art of the possible with community mapping, and overseeing our physical hub location.
