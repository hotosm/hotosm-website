According to the Global Humanitarian Overview, nearly 305 million people around the world will need humanitarian assistance and protection in 2025 due to conflicts, climate emergencies, and other drivers. Despite these daunting challenges, our open mapping communities and partners continue to rise to the occasion, navigating complexities and seizing opportunities to create tangible, measurable impact for those we serve. The pages of this Impact Report bear testimony to their dedication, resilience, and innovation.
