Raiza Pilatowsky Gruner is HOT's Senior Communications Manager. Originally from Mexico City, she has a wide range of experience on producing high quality content for impactful organizations. She is the former director of Planeteando, a platform of environmental communication for Spanish-speaking audiences, which she co-founded. She has experience producing podcasts, video essays, blogs, newsletters, performances, and environmental festivals in collaboration with various organizations like the National Geographic Society, the American Geophysical Union, the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), as well as environmental activists and land defenders from Mexico.

She holds a Bachelor's degree in Earth Science from UNAM and an MSc in Environment and Sustainable Development from University College London. She has a graduate certification studies in Science Communication and Disasters and Climate Change.
