Nicole is a total map nerd and loves public transportation maps the best. She also loves community organizing, open source projects, the iD editor, JOSM, Hugo, web development, and playing around on GitHub.

Nicole is an active member of the Community Working Group, the Open Tech and Innovation Working Group, and the Quality Control and Assurance Working Group, and has been welcoming and training new mappers with Missing Maps London since 2020. She’s been a HOT voting member since 2022 and has sat on the governance team at MapSwipe since October 2024.
In her spare time, she can be found knitting, hiking, going to art museums and film festivals, and hanging out with her beloved dog, Yuki. She currently lives in Hamburg, Germany.
