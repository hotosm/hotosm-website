Leen is the Director of Technology and Data at the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT). Her drive: connecting people through mapping for Social Good.

The HOT Technology team she leads co-creates open-source tools that empower communities to create and use geospatial data in a collaborative way. The democratization of this geospatial technology ensures that communities govern their own data, protect it, and use it to meet local needs. It enables people to actively participate in decision-making and become local change-makers through the use of community-generated data.
