Belén is a strategic engagement and partnerships specialist with over 15 years of experience in the tech and social impact sectors. With a background in geodesy and surveying and a career spanning roles at HERE Technologies and Allianz Partners, she has led global initiatives in DEI, employee engagement, and corporate social responsibility. Her expertise includes data acquisition, community development, and managing large-scale mapping and volunteer programs. Belén has built long-term collaborations with companies, and partnered with academic networks across the globe.  She is passionate about inclusive innovation, open data, and building meaningful connections that drive collective impact.
