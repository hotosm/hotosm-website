Laura Guzmán helps smart, values-driven teams make sense of complex trends, strengthen their internal systems, and communicate with clarity. Over the past fourteen years, she’s worked across technology, social impact, and organizational strategy. In that time, she has led global systems and communications for DevGlobal, served as Co-Deputy Director at The Engine Room, and supported various initiatives backed by NASA, the International Federation of the Red Cross, Amnesty International, Ford Foundation, and many others.

Earlier in her career, she worked with the Open Society Foundations, exploring how technology can both advance and threaten human rights. She later joined iMerit in Kolkata, India, helping grow a social enterprise that used data work to expand economic opportunity, and conducted urban development research in Uganda while completing her master’s at The New School. The throughline of it all has been finding ways to build and sustain systems that care for people and the living world we share.

Across her roles, Laura has helped organizations become steadier and effective. She’s guided leadership teams through restructures, built ops systems that work for the people using them, and turned ambitious ideas into executable strategy. Her work has included designing processes to strengthen cross-team collaboration, leading rebrands to sharpen business development efforts, and driving communications strategies that doubled engagement across channels.

Currently living in Portland, Oregon, Laura serves on the boards of Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team and Wonderfolk, a local youth leadership nonprofit. She is happiest when she’s deep in a book about post-capitalist futures or backpacking somewhere off-grid with a very dialed snack system.
