Krishtina joins HOT as the Senior Finance Associate for HOT's Asia Pacific Hub. She is an Associate Member of CPA Australia and has completed her Master's Degree in Business
Studies with a major in Finance/Accounts from Tribhuvan University.

She has more than 8 years of experience of working in various roles in Finance and Audit in the non-profit sector in Nepal: Feed the Future Nepal USAID Agricultural Inputs as Finance Manager, Plan International Nepal as Finance Specialist, CARE Nepal as Grants and Budget Officer, Save The Children Nepal as Internal Audit Officer to name few.
